## What the evidence says (and how it reshapes the strategy)

1. **LinkedIn/X → newsletter is the verified growth engine in this exact niche.** Every corroborated case study (Superhuman AI et al.) traces to personal social distribution converting to an owned list. Blog SEO alone is structurally weakened: AI Overviews have cut #1-position CTR ~50-58% on how-to queries.
2. **But our SEO situation is unusually cheap to win:** "install claude code" = 22,200 searches/mo at LOW competition. "claude code vs cursor" = 8,100 LOW. "claude code desktop" = 6,600 + 1,900 LOW. "claude code mcp servers" = 1,000 LOW. We have posts adjacent to all of these, currently capturing near zero. This is refresh-and-retarget work, not net-new writing.
3. **Content upgrades are the highest-evidence signup lever:** 0.54% → 4.82% opt-in in the best-verified case. Today only 5 of 36 posts have ever converted a signup; 60% of signups come from the homepage.
4. **A $99–199 self-serve course to an engaged list is the best-evidenced revenue move at our scale:** verified 2.5% conversion on engaged segments (a $999 course did $105k off 4,245 engaged subs; $99 courses produce $3k+ launches off sub-1,000 lists). Our engagement (66% open / 16.7% click) is elite, which is the variable that matters.
5. **YouTube compounds influence and AEO simultaneously:** YouTube mentions correlate with AI-answer visibility at r=0.74, the strongest signal found. Expect 12–18 months to real scale; the 2025 anti-slop policy favors genuine practitioners.
6. **AEO/LLM citations are a byproduct, not a channel** (~0.1–1% of traffic industry-wide). We get it free by doing #1 and #5.
